## Patterns across the record

- **Ideas were filed as full ticket trees before a user existed.** Decomposition outran shipping: 100+ tickets with none done within days. Act by capping ready tickets to the MVP slice and holding the rest as Later children. Shown by backseat-analyst, funding, jre, territory, route-optimizer, learninglabs, showcase, the backseat beta plan.
- **The store grows far faster than it closes, and the review queue is the real backlog.** About 425 created against 12 closed in a week; about 400 to 411 in review; cleanup shrank nothing. Act by treating review debt as Now and throttling creation, not by deduplicating. Shown by desk, consulting, jeby-us, musicalgames, management, store cleanup, pass 2, the three passes.
- **Built is not shipped: slices stop one step short of the instance people use.** Act by counting a slice as done only when it is live or installed, and putting the merge or install first. Shown by the eighteen-point review, identity work, the real-estate launch, the partner package, jeby-us.
- **The owner's own clicks and choices are the bottleneck that blocks every project.** One-minute steps, rate approvals, entity choices, 155 listed decisions and dozens of single-choice tickets wait on one person. Act by batching decisions, taking defaults under executive-decisions, and leading every report with the oldest one-minute ask. Shown by security, consulting, brand, fightplan, JRE sequencing, the drive saga, management.
- **Priority stopped meaning anything.** p0 is 27 to 38 percent of open tickets in the busiest projects and p0 plus p1 above 60 percent in others. Act by capping p0 per project and flagging inflation. Shown by desk, management, consulting, jre, algo.
- **The management project is the one that blocks all others.** It holds the weekly review, merge authority and the decision list, has 1 done of 58, and never shipped the dashboard that would show the signals. Act by making that dashboard its first Now row. Shown by management, the three passes, store cleanup.
- **MVPs were named in prose but almost never tagged, and often named as a decision or a first task rather than something a user can touch.** Only a handful of the 46 subjects carry a countable MVP. Act by requiring a literal mvp tag on one user-facing ticket per project. Shown by desk (1 of 280), writing, driver-tracking, florida-man, quorum, the eighteen-point review, the real-estate launch.
- **Where Now was followed, it worked fast.** Protect-main applied the same hour, the act-as breach fixed in a day, the sales-data bug split out the day it was flagged, unsafe data cut within a day, two sites live in pass 3; the parked and undescribed ideas that were only recorded stayed cheap. Shown by security, partner, tutoring, JRE sequencing, pass 3, quorum, florida-man, ganado, philanthropy, channel.
