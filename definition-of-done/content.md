- [ ] Every factual claim carries a source the reader can open; claims that cannot be sourced are cut or labeled opinion.
- [ ] Numbers and quotes checked against the source, not against memory.

- [ ] Read aloud once for voice (James's voice for pieces he signs); jargon expanded on first use.
