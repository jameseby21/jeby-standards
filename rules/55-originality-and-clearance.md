# Originality and clearance (Market Defense)

James's rule, 2026-11-10: originality and lawsuit prevention are inherent in every jeby Industries product, both implicitly (every session checks without being asked) and explicitly (a written record and a named Definition of Done module). James decides each case; agents surface the facts, never decide them.

**When it applies.** A new project or product; a new feature; a public name; and anything that uses someone else's work, data, name, brand, likeness, code, invention or platform. Re-check at every release, and whenever a competitor, a license or a law changes what we knew.

**The record.** Every product carries a dated Market Defense record (`templates/MARKET_DEFENSE.md`), linked from its project profile under "Market Defense". It covers:
1. **Originality.** What is new; the closest competitors and prior art; free and paid alternatives; what we do that they don't.
2. **Rights map.** Every right that touches the product and who holds it:
   - copyright (masters vs compositions, lyrics, charts and other derivatives);
   - trademarks and trade dress;
   - patents;
   - data and API licenses and terms of service;
   - open-source licenses and how far copyleft reaches;
   - publicity and likeness;
   - contracts that bind James.
3. **Our own position.** What we can protect and how: copyright, trademark, trade secret, contract or patent. And what we can't protect.
4. **Routes, item by item.** Clear; license it (from whom, typical cost); design around; replace with original, public-domain or user-supplied material; partner; or avoid. Lawful routes only: never evasion, concealment or "they won't notice".
5. **Verdicts.** Green, amber or red per item, with the sources opened and their dates. Anything that needs a lawyer is marked "needs counsel".

**What agents do.**
- Before proposing or building, read the record. If it's missing or stale, say so and propose the Market Defense ticket instead of proceeding on an assumption.
- Every amber or red item becomes a `Choose:` decision ticket for James, with the options and a recommendation. James's decision, in his words, is the clearance. Nothing is cleared by assumption.
- A name is a working name until the trademark search is done (rule 50).
- This is not legal advice. The record informs James and counsel; it never presents a legal conclusion as settled.

**Definition of Done.** A ticket that makes something public, or brings in outside works, data, names or code, names the `clearance` module.
